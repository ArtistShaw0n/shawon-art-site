ONNOROKOM PROJUKTI · DEVELOPMENT · 2026
DevelopmentLiveDesign + build

An enterprise resource planning platform for OnnoRokom Projukti Limited — designed in Figma, built and shipped to production.

Live site Repo · private Behance
Problem

What the client needed

One system for the company's core operations, replacing scattered tools with a single place to work from.

My role

Design and development, end to end

Product design in Figma through to the production build — one person from wireframe to deploy.

Decisions

What I chose, and why

  • Design the system in Figma first, then build 1:1 from it.
  • Ship to a public demo early so the client could click, not imagine.
  • Keep the information architecture flat — every module two clicks from home.
