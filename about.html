Bio

I design products and then build them. Most of it starts in Figma and ends in a front end that ships — thirty-one interface projects, ten of them carried through to code, and eighteen more that were graphic work. For OnnoRokom Projukti that meant an ERP replacing a drawer full of separate tools; for Udvash–Unmesh, a design system their portal is built from.

Under the name linearterra I draw stock graphics and sell them across five marketplaces. It is the half of the work with no client and no brief — I make what I think someone will need, then find out whether I was right.

The drawing came first. I have painted on paper since 2010 — watercolour, pastel, poster colour, coloured pencil — and that never stopped, it only moved. Now it is also Photoshop, where thirty-five digital paintings live alongside one piece drawn in Illustrator, and a camera: forty-eight frames sorted into light, water, made things, and green. Under Rakhal I write.

Dhaka, Bangladesh. This site is the one address for all of it — Behance, GitHub and the marketplaces link out from here.
