Brief to live, one pair of hands

Five stages, the same every time. Design and build by the same person — nothing is lost in a handover, and the design is the spec.

  1. 01
    Brief
    What, for whom, by when
  2. 02
    Figma
    Wireframe to approved hi-fi
  3. 03
    Build
    Next.js, 1:1 from the design
  4. 04
    Ship
    Vercel, domain, verified live
  5. 05
    Care
    Fixes, updates, next version

The design is the spec — the build is the design.
